About Toast Talent

Toast Talent is a boutique modeling agency built on refinement, individuality, and presence. We represent models with character — faces that translate effortlessly from editorial to commercial, from runway to campaign.

Our focus is quality over quantity. Every model we represent is carefully selected, professionally developed, and positioned with intention. We work closely with brands, creatives, and casting directors to deliver talent that feels current, elevated, and memorable.

Whether you are a brand seeking striking talent or a model ready to step into the industry with purpose, Toast Talent is where potential meets polish.
